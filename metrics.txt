######
Concept 1 — Why This Is Dangerous
@app.get("/blog/{num}")
def get_blog(num: int):
    REQUEST_COUNT.labels(method="GET", endpoint=f"/blog/{num}").inc()

Why this is bad

If users hit:

/blog/1
/blog/2
/blog/9999

Prometheus creates thousands of unique metrics.

This is called:

High cardinality:
Breaks Prometheus
Eats memory
Slows queries

endpoint="/blog"
